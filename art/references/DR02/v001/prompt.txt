The asset family: "DR02 buttress-root wall modules". Each module is ONE long tree root, 10-14 m long, that looks like a buttress root of the giant tree continuing outward: one end is thick (about 1.6-2.2 m across, a tall flattened plank-like buttress with a sharp ridge on top) and the other end gets thinner and dives into the soil. Several modules are laid head to tail to form a curved wall around a forest clearing. Along the sides there are thick twisted ribs and furrows that spiral slowly along the root. Deep grey-brown old bark with long ridges and furrows, pale grey lichen patches on raised ridges, darker crevices, thick dark olive-green moss on the upward-facing surfaces, a few small ferns. Three variants:
 A "high arch": the root lifts off the ground in the middle into a low hump about 3.5-4 m high, leaving a dark gap underneath (too low to walk through, about 1 m), then dives back down.
 B "low crawl": the root lies along the ground, 1.5-2 m high, gently snaking, half buried, with small side rootlets.
 C "fork": the thick end splits into two roots that separate into a long Y, each branch diving into the soil, 2.5-3 m high at the split.
