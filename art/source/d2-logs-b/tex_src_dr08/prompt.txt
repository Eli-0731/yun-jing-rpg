Do not write any code other than what is needed to copy the generated files into ./out/.

These are TEXTURES, not illustrations:
- Square, at least 1024x1024.
- Seen straight from above (orthographic, flat), filling the whole square edge to edge. No perspective, no horizon, no sky, no background, no frame, no border, no vignette, no text.
- Even, flat, soft overcast light from directly above. No strong cast shadows and no directional highlights (the game engine adds lighting). Only gentle ambient occlusion in deep cracks.
- Seamless / tileable: the left edge must continue into the right edge and the top into the bottom.
- Hand-painted Eastern xianxia fantasy game style, like the reference images: clear readable medium-size shapes, painterly but not noisy, low saturation.
